Quality & Regulatory Compliance

SWAROSH PHARMACEUTICALS PRIVATE LIMITED is strictly committed to responsible pharmaceutical distribution and adherence to applicable regulatory standards.

Adhering to Indian Drug Regulations

As a professional pharmaceutical wholesale distributor, SWAROSH maintains rigorous procedures to verify drug licenses, batch numbers, expiry dates, and proper storage environments before product dispatch.

  • Wholesale Drug License Compliance: Operations conducted under valid state regulatory licenses.
  • Good Distribution Practices (GDP): Standardized warehouse storage & clean room environment.
  • GST & Statutory Filings: Fully compliant corporate tax and invoice records.
  • Batch Traceability: Complete record maintenance for every supply transaction.
